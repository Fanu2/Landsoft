# Punjab & Haryana Revenue Terms

## Urdu – Persian – English Glossary

---

# A

## آبادی دیہ (Abadi Deh)

### English

Village habitation area.

### Meaning

Residential portion of village.

---

## آبی (Aabi)

### English

Irrigated land.

### Meaning

Land receiving irrigation from non-rain sources.

---

# B

## بنجر (Banjar)

### English

Waste land.

### Meaning

Uncultivated land.

---

## بنجر قدیم (Banjar Kadim)

### English

Old waste land.

### Meaning

Land uncultivated for many years.

---

## بنجر جدید (Banjar Jadid)

### English

New waste land.

### Meaning

Recently left uncultivated.

---

## بٹائی (Batai)

### English

Produce share rent.

### Meaning

Tenant pays landlord through crop share.

---

## بلا لگان (Bila Lagan)

### English

Without rent.

### Meaning

Possession recorded without rent liability.

---

# C

## چکوٹہ (Chakota)

### English

Fixed rent tenancy.

### Meaning

Tenant pays fixed amount rather than crop share.

---

# G

## غیر موروثی (Gair Marusi)

### English

Non-occupancy tenant.

### Meaning

Tenant lacking occupancy rights.

### Litigation Importance

Does not automatically become owner.

---

## غیر ممکن (Gair Mumkin)

### English

Non-cultivable land.

### Examples

* Road
* Pond
* Building
* Drain

---

## گرداوری (Girdawari)

### English

Crop inspection register.

### Meaning

Seasonal cultivation record.

---

# H

## حصہ دار (Hissedar)

### English

Co-sharer.

### Meaning

Joint owner of khewat.

---

# J

## جمابندی (Jamabandi)

### English

Record of Rights.

### Meaning

Primary revenue record showing ownership and possession.

---

## جمعہ مشترکہ مالکان (Jumla Mushtarka Malkan)

### English

Joint proprietary land.

### Meaning

Land jointly owned by proprietors.

---

# K

## کھسرہ نمبر (Khasra Number)

### English

Field survey number.

### Meaning

Unique identification of land parcel.

---

## کھتونی (Khatoni)

### English

Cultivation account.

---

## کھوات (Khewat)

### English

Ownership account.

---

## خودکاشت (Khudkasht)

### English

Self-cultivated by owner.

### Litigation Importance

Often used to rebut tenancy claims.

---

## خانہ کاشت (Khanakasht)

### English

Owner cultivation.

### Meaning

Historical expression indicating self-cultivation.

---

# M

## مقبوضہ مالکان (Makbuja Malkan)

### English

Possession of proprietors.

### Litigation Importance

Strong evidence against tenancy claims.

---

## مراسی / موروثی (Maurusi)

### English

Occupancy tenant.

### Meaning

Protected hereditary tenant.

---

## مثل حقیات (Misal Haqiat)

### English

Record of Rights.

### Meaning

Historical settlement record.

---

## انتقال (Mutation / Intiqal)

### English

Mutation.

### Meaning

Revenue entry reflecting transfer or succession.

---

# P

## پٹواری (Patwari)

### English

Village revenue official.

---

## تقسیم (Partition)

### English

Partition.

### Meaning

Separation of joint holding.

---

# R

## روزنامچہ واقعاتی (Roznamcha Waqiati)

### English

Daily diary.

### Meaning

Patwari's daily record.

---

# S

## سند تقسیم (Sanad Taksim)

### English

Instrument of partition.

### Meaning

Final partition order.

---

## شجرہ (Shajra)

### English

Village map.

---

## شملات دیہ (Shamlat Deh)

### English

Village common land.

---

# T

## تحصیلدار (Tehsildar)

### English

Revenue officer.

---

## تتیمہ (Tatima)

### English

Supplementary map.

### Meaning

Detailed map prepared for dispute or demarcation.

---

# W

## واجب العرض (Wajib-ul-Arz)

### English

Record of village customs.

### Meaning

Settlement record of customs, easements and practices.

---

# Z

## زمیندار (Zamindar)

### English

Landholder.

---

## زیلدار (Zaildar)

### English

Historical revenue functionary.
