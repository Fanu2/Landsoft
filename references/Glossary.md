# Glossary of Punjab & Haryana Revenue Terms

## Land Records, Tenancy, Partition & Consolidation

---

# A

## Aks Shajra

Official village map showing:

* Khasra numbers
* Boundaries
* Roads
* Watercourses

### Litigation Importance

Used in:

* Demarcation
* Encroachment
* Boundary disputes

---

## Assistant Collector

Revenue officer exercising powers under revenue laws.

Usually first authority in:

* Partition cases
* Mutation disputes
* Revenue proceedings

---

# B

## Bachat Land

Land left over after consolidation.

### Litigation Importance

Frequently involved in:

* Panchayat disputes
* Jumla Mushtarka Malkan cases

---

## Banjar

Uncultivated land.

---

## Banjar Jadid

Recently uncultivated land.

---

## Banjar Kadim

Land uncultivated for a long period.

---

## Batai

Rent paid as share of produce.

### Example

50% wheat crop to landlord.

---

## Bila Lagan

Without rent.

### Litigation Importance

Frequently appears in Gair Marusi disputes.

---

# C

## Chakota

Fixed rent arrangement.

Usually monetary rent.

---

## Collector

Revenue authority hearing appeals from Assistant Collector.

---

## Consolidation

Process of rearranging fragmented agricultural holdings.

---

# D

## Demarcation

Official determination of land boundaries on the ground.

---

## Dohlidar

Person holding land under a traditional grant.

### Litigation Importance

Special category often found in old records.

---

# F

## Financial Commissioner

Highest revenue authority in Punjab and Haryana.

---

# G

## Gair Dakhildar

Person without lawful possession rights.

---

## Gair Marusi

Non-occupancy tenant.

### Important

Does not automatically mean:

* Owner
* Occupancy tenant
* Adverse possessor

---

## Gair Mumkin

Land incapable of cultivation.

Examples:

* Roads
* Buildings
* Ponds

---

## Girdawari

Seasonal record of cultivation.

---

# H

## Hissedar

Co-sharer.

---

# J

## Jamabandi

Record of Rights.

Most important operational revenue document.

---

## Jumla Mushtarka Malkan

Land owned jointly by proprietors.

### Litigation Importance

Central issue in consolidation disputes.

---

# K

## Kanal

Unit of area.

1 Kanal = 20 Marla

---

## Karam

Traditional unit of length.

1 Karam = 5.5 Feet

---

## Khasra Number

Survey number assigned to a field parcel.

---

## Khasra Girdawari

Seasonal cultivation record.

---

## Khewat

Ownership account number.

---

## Khatoni

Cultivation account number.

---

## Khudkasht

Land cultivated by proprietor himself.

### Litigation Importance

Can weaken tenancy claims.

---

## Khanakasht

Historical expression generally indicating self-cultivation.

---

# M

## Makbuja Malkan

Possession of proprietors.

### Litigation Importance

Important evidence against tenancy claims.

---

## Marla

Unit of area.

1 Marla = 272.25 sq. ft.

---

## Maurusi

Occupancy tenant.

Usually stronger rights than Gair Marusi.

---

## Misal Haqiat

Historical settlement record.

One of the most valuable revenue documents.

---

## Mutation

Revenue record of change.

### Important Rule

Mutation does not create title.

---

## Muqarraridar

Holder paying fixed assessment.

Found in historical records.

---

# N

## Naib Tehsildar

Subordinate revenue officer.

---

# O

## Occupancy Tenant

Protected tenant recognized by law.

May enjoy hereditary rights.

---

# P

## Panchayat Land

Land vested in Gram Panchayat.

Often governed by Village Common Lands legislation.

---

## Partition

Separation of joint holdings into distinct holdings.

---

## Patwari

Village-level revenue official.

Maintains:

* Jamabandi
* Girdawari
* Roznamcha

---

# R

## Rect Number

Rectangle number used after consolidation.

---

## Roznamcha

Daily diary maintained by Patwari.

---

# S

## Sanad Taksim

Final partition instrument.

---

## Sarsahi

Traditional area unit.

1 Sarsahi = 30.25 sq. ft.

---

## Shamlat Deh

Village common land.

---

## Settlement Record

Historical revenue record prepared during settlement operations.

---

# T

## Tatima

Detailed map prepared for a particular dispute.

---

## Tehsildar

Revenue officer exercising important statutory powers.

---

# V

## Vesting

Transfer of ownership by operation of law.

Example:

Occupancy tenants becoming owners under vesting legislation.

---

# W

## Wajib-ul-Arz

Village custom record.

### Contains

* Customs
* Water rights
* Grazing rights
* Tenancy practices

---

# Z

## Zaildar

Historical revenue functionary.

Found in older records.
