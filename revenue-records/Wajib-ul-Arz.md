# Wajib-ul-Arz Guide

---

# What is Wajib-ul-Arz?

Village administration and custom record prepared during settlement.

---

# Typical Subjects

* Grazing rights
* Water rights
* Village customs
* Tenancy customs
* Common land usage

---

# Litigation Importance

Courts frequently rely upon Wajib-ul-Arz when:

* Custom is pleaded
* Occupancy rights are claimed
* Village practices are disputed

---

# Common Uses

## Occupancy Rights

Evidence of customary tenancy.

---

## Common Land

Evidence regarding village usage.

---

## Easement Rights

Access, pathways and water use.

---

# Evidentiary Value

Very high historical value.

Should always be reviewed alongside:

* Misal Haqiat
* Settlement Records
* Old Jamabandis
