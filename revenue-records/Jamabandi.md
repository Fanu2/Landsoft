# Jamabandi Guide

## Punjab & Haryana Revenue Records

---

# What is Jamabandi?

Jamabandi is the Record of Rights maintained by the Revenue Department.

Traditionally revised every four years.

It records:

* Ownership
* Possession
* Cultivation
* Revenue liability
* Rent
* Mutation references

---

# Importance

Jamabandi is usually the first document examined in:

* Partition cases
* Ownership disputes
* Gair Marusi litigation
* Occupancy claims
* Mutation disputes

---

# Typical Columns

## Khewat Number

Ownership account number.

---

## Khatoni Number

Cultivation account number.

---

## Khasra Number

Survey field number.

---

## Area

Land area of the khasra.

---

## Owner Column

Records proprietors.

---

## Possession/Cultivation Column

Shows who actually cultivates.

---

## Rent Column

Extremely important.

May contain:

* Batai
* Chakota
* Bila Lagan

---

# Litigation Value

Jamabandi carries a presumption of correctness.

However:

* Presumption is rebuttable.
* It is not title itself.

---

# Common Entries

## Makbuja Malkan

Possession of proprietors.

---

## Gair Marusi

Non-occupancy tenant.

---

## Maurusi

Occupancy tenant.

---

## Bila Lagan

Without rent.

---

# Practical Checklist

Always compare:

* Current Jamabandi
* Previous Jamabandi
* Earliest available Jamabandi

Changes often reveal the true dispute.
