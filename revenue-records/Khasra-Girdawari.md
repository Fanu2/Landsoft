# Khasra Girdawari Guide

---

# What is Khasra Girdawari?

Seasonal cultivation record maintained by Patwari.

Records:

* Possession
* Crop
* Cultivator

---

# Seasons

## Kharif

Monsoon crops.

---

## Rabi

Winter crops.

---

# Purpose

Shows actual cultivation.

Useful in:

* Possession disputes
* Tenancy disputes
* Crop compensation claims

---

# Important Limitation

Girdawari does not prove ownership.

It only records cultivation and possession.

---

# Common Entries

## Khud Kasht

Owner cultivation.

---

## Gair Marusi

Tenant cultivation.

---

## Makbuja Malkan

Cultivated by proprietors.

---

# Litigation Value

Strong evidence of possession.

Weak evidence of title.
