# Mutation Guide

## Punjab & Haryana Revenue Records

---

# What is Mutation?

Mutation records changes affecting revenue records.

Examples:

* Sale
* Gift
* Inheritance
* Court decree
* Exchange

---

# Important Principle

Mutation does NOT create title.

Mutation does NOT extinguish title.

Mutation only updates revenue records.

---

# Common Mutation Types

## Inheritance Mutation

Death of owner.

---

## Sale Mutation

Based upon registered sale deed.

---

## Court Decree Mutation

Based upon judicial order.

---

## Partition Mutation

After partition proceedings.

---

# Litigation Strategy

Always obtain complete mutation chain.

Many disputes become clear after reviewing mutation history.

---

# Common Mistake

Relying solely upon mutation.

Title must be independently established.
