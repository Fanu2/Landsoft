# Misal Haqiat Guide

---

# What is Misal Haqiat?

Historical settlement record.

One of the most valuable documents in Punjab and Haryana revenue litigation.

---

# Why Important?

Many disputes involving:

* Occupancy rights
* Gair Marusi
* Khudkasht
* Proprietary rights

are decided by old settlement records.

---

# Typical Information

* Ownership
* Cultivation
* Tenancy
* Revenue assessment

---

# Evidentiary Value

Higher than:

* Current Jamabandi
* Current Girdawari

Often treated as foundational evidence.

---

# Practical Advice

Always obtain Misal Haqiat before:

* Filing tenancy litigation
* Claiming occupancy rights
* Contesting historical possession
