# Roznamcha and Aks Shajra Guide

## Punjab & Haryana Revenue Records

---

# Introduction

Among all revenue records, the most overlooked are:

1. Roznamcha Waqiati
2. Aks Shajra
3. Tatima Shajra

Yet these documents often decide disputes involving:

* Possession
* Partition
* Encroachments
* Pathways
* Irrigation channels
* Boundary disputes
* Gair Marusi cultivation
* Co-sharer possession

---

# Part I – Roznamcha Waqiati

## What is Roznamcha?

Roznamcha Waqiati is the daily diary maintained by the Patwari.

It records important events relating to:

* Land
* Cultivation
* Possession
* Revenue administration

---

# Legal Importance

Courts frequently rely upon Roznamcha entries because they are contemporaneous records.

A Roznamcha entry often carries greater evidentiary value than later oral testimony.

---

# Common Entries

## Change of Possession

Example:

Person enters possession of land.

---

## Change in Cultivation

Example:

Different cultivator starts farming.

---

## Patwari Inspection

Example:

Field inspection conducted.

---

## Partition Proceedings

Example:

Demarcation completed.

---

## Mutation Proceedings

Example:

Inheritance mutation sanctioned.

---

# Why Lawyers Should Read Roznamcha

Many disputes fail because parties only examine:

* Jamabandi
* Girdawari

but ignore Roznamcha.

Roznamcha frequently explains:

* Why entries changed.
* When possession changed.
* Whether Patwari actually visited site.

---

# Important Revenue Manual References

Roznamcha entries are often linked with:

* Girdawari inspections
* Mutation proceedings
* Demarcation proceedings

---

# Practical Litigation Use

When challenging:

* Girdawari changes
* Possession entries
* Mutation entries

always obtain relevant Roznamcha entries.

---

# Part II – Aks Shajra

## What is Aks Shajra?

Aks Shajra is the official village map.

It shows:

* Khasra numbers
* Boundaries
* Roads
* Water channels
* Abadi areas

---

# Importance

Aks Shajra is essential in:

* Boundary disputes
* Encroachment disputes
* Partition disputes
* Demarcation proceedings

---

# Typical Features

## Khasra Numbers

Each field parcel.

---

## Rectangles

Common in consolidation villages.

---

## Watercourses

Khals and channels.

---

## Roads

Village paths and public roads.

---

## Abadi

Residential areas.

---

# Reading Aks Shajra

Step 1

Identify village.

---

Step 2

Identify rectangle number.

---

Step 3

Identify khasra number.

---

Step 4

Locate adjoining khasras.

---

Step 5

Check access paths.

---

# Common Litigation Issues

## Encroachment

Whether one party has crossed the boundary.

---

## Access Disputes

Whether pathway exists.

---

## Watercourse Disputes

Whether irrigation channel exists.

---

## Partition

Whether allotted parcel is identifiable.

---

# Part III – Tatima Shajra

## What is Tatima?

Tatima is a detailed map prepared for a specific dispute.

Usually prepared during:

* Partition
* Demarcation
* Court proceedings

---

# Purpose

To identify:

* Exact disputed land
* Boundaries
* Encroachments

---

# Importance

Tatima often becomes the most important map in litigation.

---

# Part V – Consolidation Maps

## Importance

Consolidation often changes field locations.

Old khasra numbers may not match current numbers.

---

# Always Obtain

* Pre-consolidation map

* Consolidation scheme

# Part VI – GPS vs Revenue Maps

## Modern Issue

Landowners frequently compare:

GPS measurement

with

Revenue records.

---

# Important Rule

Revenue ownership is determined by official records.

GPS is only evidentiary.

---

# Practical Measurement Workflow

1. Obtain Aks Shajra.
2. Obtain Tatima.
3. Measure boundaries.
4. Compare with GPS survey.
5. Verify field position.
