## Kanal

---

## Acre

1 Acre = 8 Kanal

1 Hectare = 2.471 Acres

# Rectangle Area

Formula:

Area = Length \times Width

---

# Triangle Area

Formula:

Area = \frac{1}{2}bh

---

# Heron's Formula

For three known sides:

A=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}

where:

s = (a+b+c)/2

---

# Trapezium Area

Area = \frac{1}{2}(a+b)h

---
