# Rasta, Watercourses and Easement Rights

## Punjab & Haryana Revenue Litigation Handbook

---

# Introduction

A large percentage of rural litigation in Punjab and Haryana concerns:

* Access to agricultural land
* Village paths (Rasta)
* Watercourses (Khal)
* Irrigation channels
* Easement rights

Many disputes are wrongly filed as ownership cases when the real issue is access or irrigation.

---

# Chapter 1 – What is a Rasta?

## Meaning

A Rasta is a pathway used for access.

May be:

* Public
* Village common
* Private
* Easementary

---

# Common Disputes

## Blocked Access

Neighbor blocks pathway.

---

## Encroachment

Construction over pathway.

---

## Denial of Use

Long-used path suddenly obstructed.

---

# Important Question

Ownership and right of use are different concepts.

A person may have a right of passage without ownership.

---

# Chapter 2 – Easement Rights

## Meaning

Right enjoyed over another person's land.

---

## Common Examples

### Right of Way

Access through another's land.

---

### Water Rights

Use of irrigation channels.

---

### Drainage Rights

Flow of water through adjoining land.

---

# Essential Elements

1. Dominant Heritage
2. Servient Heritage
3. Beneficial Enjoyment
