# Introduction

Most revenue cases are won or lost before litigation begins.

The quality of investigation is usually more important than the quality of arguments.

This handbook provides a systematic workflow for:

* Lawyers
* Landowners
* Revenue Consultants
* Researchers
* Property Purchasers
