# Haryana Land Records Manual – Case Law Digest

## Punjab & Haryana High Court and Revenue Law Principles

---

# Introduction

The Haryana Land Records Manual is not merely an administrative handbook.

Courts frequently examine whether:

* Patwari followed prescribed procedure.
* Revenue authorities issued notice.
* Girdawari changes were properly made.
* Mutations were legally sanctioned.
* Demarcations were properly conducted.
* Partition proceedings complied with revenue law.

This digest links common litigation issues with judicial principles.

---

# Chapter 1 – Jamabandi Entries

## Principle

Jamabandi carries a presumption of correctness.

However:

* Presumption is rebuttable.
* Jamabandi is evidence.
* Jamabandi is not title itself.

---

## Litigation Rule

Always compare:

* Current Jamabandi
* Previous Jamabandi
* Mutation chain

Revenue authorities maintain Jamabandi as the Record of Rights.

---

# Chapter 2 – Mutation Entries

## Principle

Mutation does not create ownership.

Mutation reflects changes arising from:

* Sale
* Inheritance
* Gift
* Court decree
* Partition

Mutation updates the Jamabandi but is not itself a title document.

---

## Common Litigation Issues

### Mutation Without Notice

Ground for challenge.

---

### Mutation Contrary to Title

Revenue entry cannot override lawful title.

---

### Fraudulent Mutation

Requires examination of:

* Mutation register
* Statements
* Supporting documents

---

# Chapter 3 – Khasra Girdawari

## Principle

Khasra Girdawari records cultivation and possession.

It is important evidence of possession but is not conclusive proof of ownership.

---

## Judicial Trend

Courts repeatedly scrutinize:

* Whether inspection occurred.
* Whether notice was given.
* Whether correction proceedings followed procedure.

Applications for correction of Khasra Girdawari frequently arise before Assistant Collectors and are regularly examined by the Punjab & Haryana High Court.

# Chapter 5 – Roznamcha Waqiati

## Principle

Roznamcha explains revenue actions.

Whenever:

* Possession changes
* Inspection occurs
* Mutation proceedings commence

supporting Roznamcha entries should exist.

---

## Litigation Strategy

If an entry changes suddenly:

check corresponding Roznamcha.

Absence of supporting entries may weaken the change.
