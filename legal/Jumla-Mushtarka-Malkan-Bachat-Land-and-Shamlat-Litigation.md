# Introduction

Some of the most complex and valuable land disputes in Punjab and Haryana involve:

* Jumla Mushtarka Malkan
* Mustarka Malkan Hasab Rasad Khewat
* Bachat Land
* Shamlat Deh
* Common Purpose Land

Many cases involve land worth crores of rupees and often arise decades after consolidation.

---

# Chapter 1 – Historical Background

## Before Consolidation

Villages often contained:

* Individual holdings
* Common land
* Grazing land
* Pathways
* Watercourses

---

## After Consolidation

Land was reorganized.

Certain portions were reserved for:

* Village common purposes
* Roads
* Schools
* Panchayat use
* Watercourses

# Chapter 2 – What is Jumla Mushtarka Malkan?

## Meaning

Joint ownership of proprietors.

---

## Typical Entry

```text
Jumla Mushtarka Malkan Wa Digar Haqdaran Hasab Rasad Raqba
```

---

## Practical Meaning

Land jointly owned by proprietors according to their shares.

---

# Litigation Question

Does ownership vest in:

* Panchayat?
* Proprietary body?
* Individual shareholders?

---

# Chapter 3 – Mustarka Malkan Hasab Rasad Khewat

## Meaning

Ownership according to share in the Khewat.

---

## Example

Khewat:

A = 1/2

B = 1/4

C = 1/4

Common land ownership follows same ratio.

---

# Importance

Determines entitlement in:

* Compensation
* Acquisition
* Partition
* Possession disputes
