# Introduction

Modern revenue records in Punjab and Haryana cannot be fully understood without understanding the Settlement System.

Many concepts originate from settlement operations:

* Jamabandi
* Misal Haqiat
* Wajib-ul-Arz
* Khewat
* Khatoni
* Occupancy Rights
* Village Customs

---

# Chapter 1 – What Was Settlement?

Settlement was the process through which Government:

* Measured land
* Assessed revenue
* Recorded ownership
* Recorded cultivation
* Recorded customs
