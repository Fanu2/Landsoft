# Dealing with Patwari, Kanungo and Tehsildar

## Practical Accountability Handbook for Landowners and Litigants in Haryana & Punjab

---

# Introduction

Most landowners believe their biggest challenge is the law.

In reality, the biggest challenge is often:

* No receipt issued.
* No record of visit.
* No written response.
* No file movement.
* Endless verbal assurances.

The purpose of this handbook is to create an evidence trail so that every interaction becomes verifiable.

---

# Final Rule

The objective is not merely to submit an application.

The objective is to create a documentary trail that can later be produced before:

* Revenue Authorities
* Deputy Commissioner
* Financial Commissioner
* High Court
* Vigilance Authorities

If an event is not documented, proving it later becomes difficult.
