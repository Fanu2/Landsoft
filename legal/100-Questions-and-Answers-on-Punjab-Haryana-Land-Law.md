# 100 Questions and Answers on Punjab & Haryana Land Law

## Q1. Does mutation create ownership?

No. Mutation is a fiscal entry and does not itself create title.

---

## Q2. Is Jamabandi proof of ownership?

Jamabandi carries a presumption of correctness but is not conclusive proof of title.

---

## Q3. Can a Patwari decide ownership?

No.

Patwari records revenue entries but cannot adjudicate title.

---

## Q4. Does long possession automatically make a person owner?

No.

Possession alone does not create ownership.

---

## Q5. Can a Gair Marusi become owner?

Not automatically.

Ownership depends on statutory rights, historical records and evidence.

---

## Q6. Is Girdawari proof of ownership?

No.

Girdawari primarily records cultivation and possession.

---

## Q7. Can a co-sharer sell land?

Yes.

A co-sharer can transfer his share.

---

## Q8. Can a co-sharer sell a specific killa?

Yes, but rights remain subject to partition.

---

## Q9. What is Khewat?

Ownership account.

---

## Q10. What is Khatoni?

Cultivation account.

---

## Q11. What is Khasra Number?

Field survey number.

---

## Q12. What is Sanad Taksim?

Final partition instrument.

---

## Q13. Can mutation be challenged?

Yes.

---

## Q14. Can Jamabandi be corrected?

Yes, through appropriate proceedings.

---

## Q15. Can Girdawari be corrected?

Yes.

---

## Q16. What is Misal Haqiat?

Historical settlement record.

---

## Q17. What is Wajib-ul-Arz?

Village custom record.

---

## Q18. What is Roznamcha?

Patwari's daily diary.

---

## Q19. What is Aks Shajra?

Village map.

---

## Q20. What is Tatima?

Detailed dispute map.
