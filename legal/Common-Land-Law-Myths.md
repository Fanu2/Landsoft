# Common Land Law Myths

## Punjab & Haryana Revenue Law

---

# Myth 1

## Mutation Means Ownership

### Reality

Mutation is a fiscal entry.

Ownership comes from:

* Inheritance
* Sale Deed
* Court Decree
* Law

---

# Myth 2

## 30 Years Possession Means Ownership

### Reality

Long possession alone does not create ownership.

Legal requirements must be satisfied.

---

# Myth 3

## Gair Marusi Automatically Becomes Owner

### Reality

False.

Gair Marusi means non-occupancy tenant.

Ownership depends on facts and law.

---

# Myth 4

## Registry of Specific Killa Gives Ownership of That Exact Killa

### Reality

In a joint khewat, purchaser often steps into shoes of co-sharer.

Final location may depend on partition.

---

# Myth 5

## Patwari Can Decide Ownership

### Reality

Patwari records entries.

Patwari does not determine title.

---

# Myth 6

## Girdawari Proves Ownership

### Reality

Girdawari proves cultivation and possession.

Not ownership.

---

# Myth 7

## Mutation Cannot Be Challenged

### Reality

Mutations are frequently challenged.

---

# Myth 8

## Possession Equals Ownership

### Reality

Possession and ownership are different concepts.

---

# Myth 9

## Revenue Records Are Always Correct

### Reality

Revenue entries carry presumption but can be rebutted.

---

# Myth 10

## Old Oral Family Settlement Is Always Valid

### Reality

Requires proof.

Often creates litigation.

---

# Myth 11

## Shamlat Land Can Be Freely Sold

### Reality

Many statutory restrictions apply.

---

# Myth 12

## Partition Ends All Disputes

### Reality

Disputes often continue regarding:

* Possession
* Boundaries
* Access
* Irrigation

---

# Myth 13

## Demarcation Is Final Forever

### Reality

Faulty demarcation can be challenged.

---

# Myth 14

## Co-Sharer Can Sell More Than His Share

### Reality

A co-sharer cannot transfer better title than he possesses.

---

# Myth 15

## Revenue Officers Can Ignore Consolidation Records

### Reality

Consolidation records are often decisive.

---

# Most Dangerous Myths

1. Mutation = Ownership
2. Possession = Ownership
3. Gair Marusi = Owner
4. Registry of Killa = Absolute Ownership
5. Patwari Decides Title

These myths generate a large percentage of rural land litigation.
