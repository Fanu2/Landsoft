## Section-by-Section Commentary for Punjab & Haryana

# Introduction

The Punjab Land Revenue Act, 1887 is the foundation of revenue administration in Punjab and Haryana.

Almost every rural land dispute eventually touches one or more provisions relating to:

* Revenue records
* Mutation
* Partition
* Revenue officers
* Appeals
* Demarcation
* Record of rights

Understanding this Act is essential for:

* Lawyers
* Revenue officers
* Landowners
* Property purchasers
* Developers

# Part XIII – Most Important Legal Principles

## Principle 1

Mutation does not create title.

---

## Principle 2

Jamabandi carries rebuttable presumption.

---

## Principle 3

Girdawari proves possession, not ownership.

---

## Principle 4

Patwari cannot determine title.

---

## Principle 5

Co-sharer owns every inch of joint property.

---

## Principle 6

Partition converts joint ownership into separate ownership.

## Principle 7

Revenue entries can be challenged.

## Principle 8

Historical records often outweigh recent entries.
