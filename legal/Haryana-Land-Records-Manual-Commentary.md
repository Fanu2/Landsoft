# Haryana Land Records Manual Commentary

## Practical Guide for Revenue Officers, Lawyers and Landowners

---

# Introduction

The Haryana Land Records Manual governs the day-to-day functioning of the revenue administration.

Most disputes involving:

* Jamabandi
* Mutation
* Girdawari
* Demarcation
* Partition
* Possession

ultimately depend upon procedures prescribed in the Manual.

Many court cases are won or lost because revenue officials either followed or violated these instructions.

# Chapter 2 – Duties of Patwari

## Record Maintenance

Maintain:

* Jamabandi
* Mutation Register
* Khasra Girdawari
* Roznamcha
* Field Book

---

## Field Inspection

Patwari must:

* Visit fields
* Verify cultivation
* Record actual possession

---

## Importance

Many litigation disputes arise from failure to conduct proper field inspections.

---

# Chapter 3 – Khasra Girdawari

## Purpose

Record actual cultivation.

---

## Inspection Requirements

Patwari must conduct seasonal inspections.

---

## Common Errors

### Paper Entries

Without actual inspection.

---

### Political Influence

Improper possession entries.

---

## Litigation Strategy

Always examine:

* Date of entry
* Supporting Roznamcha
* Crop consistency

---

# Chapter 4 – Roznamcha Waqiati

## Purpose

Daily diary of Patwari.

---

## Must Record

* Possession changes
* Mutation events
* Inspections
* Demarcations

---

## Practical Rule

Whenever a Girdawari entry changes:

Check corresponding Roznamcha entry.
