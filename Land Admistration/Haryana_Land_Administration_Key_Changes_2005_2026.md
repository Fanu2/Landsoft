# Haryana Land Administration – Key Changes (2005–2026)

## Purpose
This note summarizes the most important developments in Haryana's land administration system from the perspective of a landowner, investor, developer, or stakeholder.

---

## 2005–2015: Computerization of Land Records

### What Changed
- Jamabandi and land records were progressively digitized.
- Online access to revenue records became available.
- Reduced dependence on physical record rooms.

### Why It Matters
- Faster title verification.
- Easier access to ownership records.
- Improved transparency.

---

## 2016–2018: WEB-HALRIS Integration

### What Changed
Integration of:
- Registration
- Mutation
- Jamabandi
- Khasra Girdawari
- E-stamping

into a unified digital platform.

### Why It Matters
- Registry and land records became linked.
- Reduced chances of transaction-related fraud.
- Faster processing of ownership changes.

---

## 2021: Jamabandi Format Revision

### What Changed
- Ownership details standardized.
- Improved recording of owner identity and shares.
- Better tracing of title history.

### Why It Matters
- Easier due diligence.
- Reduced ambiguity in ownership records.

---

## 2022–2025: SVAMITVA & Abadi Deh Reforms

### What Changed
- Drone surveys of village Abadi Deh areas.
- Property boundary mapping.
- Preparation of Record of Rights.
- Issuance of ownership certificates.

### Why It Matters
- Village residential properties obtained stronger legal recognition.
- Improved marketability and mortgageability of properties.
- Better boundary certainty.

---

## 2025–2026: Haryana Abadi Deh Act

### Most Important Reform

The Haryana Abadi Deh (Vesting, Recording and Resolving of Ownership Rights) Act, 2025:

- Formally recognizes ownership rights of occupiers in Abadi Deh areas.
- Creates legally recognized ownership records.
- Provides SVAMITVA Rights Certificates and title documents.
- Establishes objection, appeal, review, and revision procedures.
- Gives statutory backing to earlier SVAMITVA surveys.

### Why It Matters
This is arguably the most significant rural property-rights reform in Haryana in decades.

---

## 2024–2025: Paperless Registration

### What Changed
- Digital workflows for property registration.
- Reduced paperwork.
- Online monitoring and tracking.

### Why It Matters
- Faster transactions.
- Better audit trail.
- Lower administrative friction.

---

## 2025–2026: Standardization of Revenue Entries

### What Changed
- Uniform naming and classification of government land.
- Improved record consistency.

### Why It Matters
- Easier title verification.
- Reduced ownership disputes.

---

## 2026: Digital Field Administration

### What Changed
- GPS-enabled tablets for Patwaris and Kanungos.
- Increased digital field operations.

### Why It Matters
- Better documentation.
- Faster surveys and demarcation.
- Reduced scope for manual manipulation.

---

# Top 5 Changes Every Stakeholder Should Track

1. SVAMITVA & Abadi Deh ownership recognition.
2. Haryana Abadi Deh Act, 2025.
3. HALRIS integration of registration and land records.
4. Automatic mutation workflows.
5. Standardized Jamabandi records.

---

# Practical Checklist

For every property:

- Verify Jamabandi entries.
- Verify mutation status.
- Check GIS/survey records.
- Confirm SVAMITVA coverage (if applicable).
- Verify ownership shares.
- Check pending litigation or objections.
- Confirm physical possession matches records.
